      <h2 id="faculty-title">Professional exchange and research access</h2>
      <p>Faculty affiliated with the University of New Mexico benefit from access to the consortium’s professional networks, workshops, and research resources. NCAIS brings together scholars from member institutions who work on Indigenous history, Native studies, borderlands, and related fields.</p>
      <p>These opportunities support faculty research agendas through collaborations, invited talks, conference participation, and sustained engagement with the Newberry’s collections and the broader scholarly community.</p>
