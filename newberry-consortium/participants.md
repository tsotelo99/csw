      <h2 id="participants-title">A network of scholars and researchers</h2>
      <p>Participants in the Newberry Library Consortium in American Indian Studies come from member institutions, graduate programs, and research communities across the United States and beyond. Their work energizes the consortium’s workshops, institutes, and conferences.</p>
      <p>For UNM students and faculty, participation offers a chance to meet scholars working on Indigenous histories, community memory, borderlands, and transnational Indigenous studies. These engagements help build professional relationships and advance new research questions.</p>

    <h2 id="participants-details-title">Collaborative and interdisciplinary exchange</h2>
    <p>The consortium supports an intellectual environment that is collaborative, interdisciplinary, and deeply grounded in Indigenous scholarship. It is a place where graduate students, faculty, and visiting scholars can exchange ideas and strengthen their work in conversation with one another.</p>
    <p>Across the consortium, participants contribute to a broader understanding of Indigenous communities, place, memory, movement, and historical change.</p>
