      <h2 id="participants-title">A community built through exchange</h2>
      <p>Participants in the Newberry Library Consortium in American Indian Studies come from member institutions, graduate programs, and research communities across the United States and beyond. Their work energizes the consortium’s workshops, institutes, and conferences.</p>
      <p>For UNM students and faculty, participation offers a chance to meet scholars working on Indigenous histories, community memory, borderlands, and transnational Indigenous studies. These engagements help build professional relationships and advance new research questions.</p>
