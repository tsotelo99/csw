      <h2 id="mission-title">Research, collaboration, and Indigenous scholarship</h2>
      <p>The University of New Mexico joined the Newberry Library Consortium in American Indian Studies (NCAIS) in 2010. Since then, faculty and students have benefited from the consortium’s workshops, institutes, conferences, fellowships, and access to the Newberry Library’s exceptional collections in American Indian and Indigenous studies.</p>
      <p>NCAIS supports a community of scholars, graduate students, and researchers across member institutions. Through sustained programming and collaborative opportunities, it creates time and space for intellectual exchange, training, and scholarly development.</p>

    <p class="about-eyebrow">About the consortium</p>
    <h2 id="history-title">A long-standing research community</h2>
    <p>In June 2008, the Newberry inaugurated a new Consortium in American Indian Studies. NCAIS began accepting members in the summer and launched its programs in July 2009.</p>
    <p>The consortium draws on the Newberry’s world-renowned collections in American Indian and Indigenous studies and supports a rich array of programmatic opportunities.</p>
    <p>The Newberry is an independent research library in Chicago with an exceptional collection of rare books, maps, music, manuscripts, and other printed materials spanning six centuries. Its staff provides award-winning service and supports a broad range of research, teaching, and public programming.</p>
