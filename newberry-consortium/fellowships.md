      <h2 id="fellowships-title">Research support for students and faculty</h2>
      <p>The Newberry Library Consortium in American Indian Studies provides graduate students and faculty with opportunities to pursue research, teaching, and collaborative scholarship grounded in Indigenous studies.</p>
      <p>These fellowships and programmatic opportunities are designed to support scholars who work with Indigenous communities, historical inquiry, and the broader intellectual life of American Indian and Indigenous studies.</p>
