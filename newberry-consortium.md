      <h2 id="overview-title">A local, editable page for the consortium</h2>
      <p>The University of New Mexico joined the Newberry Library Consortium in American Indian Studies in 2010.</p>
      <p>Since then, faculty and students have used the consortium’s annual workshops, institutes, conferences, and fellowships to build research networks and pursue new scholarship in American Indian and Indigenous studies.</p>
      <p>Through this partnership, UNM scholars have gained access to the Newberry Library’s collections and the resources of the D’Arcy McNickle Center.</p>

    <p class="about-eyebrow">About the consortium</p>
    <h2 id="about-title">A research community rooted in Indigenous scholarship</h2>
    <p>In June 2008, the Newberry inaugurated a new Consortium in American Indian Studies. It began accepting members in the summer and launched its programs in July 2009.</p>
    <p>The consortium draws on the Newberry’s world-renowned collections in American Indian and Indigenous studies and creates opportunities for faculty, graduate students, and researchers to collaborate across institutions.</p>
    <p>The Newberry is an independent research library in Chicago with exceptional holdings in rare books, maps, music, manuscripts, and other printed materials spanning six centuries.</p>
