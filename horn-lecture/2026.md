      <p>The Center for the Southwest at the University of New Mexico is pleased to welcome Dr. Elizabeth Ellis for the 2026 C. Ruth and Calvin P. Horn Lecture in Western History and Culture.</p>
      <p>Dr. Ellis teaches early American and Native American history. Her research focuses on diplomacy, borderlands, cross-cultural exchange, and Indigenous politics. Her first book, <em>The Great Power of Small Nations: Indigenous Diplomacy in the Gulf South</em> (2022), examines the formation of Native American nations in the Lower Mississippi Valley.</p>
