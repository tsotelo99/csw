      <p>The Center for the Southwest at the University of New Mexico is pleased to welcome Dr. Rachek St. John for the 2027 C. Ruth and Calvin P. Horn Lecture in Western History and Culture.</p>
      <p>Rachel St John’s research focuses on North American history with a particular emphasis on state-formation and nation-building in the nineteenth century. Her first book, Line in the Sand: A History of the Western U.S.-Mexico Border, was published by Princeton University Press in early 2011. Her current book project, <em>The Imagined States of America: The Unmanifest History of Nineteenth-century North America,</em>explores the diverse range of nation-building projects that emerged across the continent in the 19th century. Originally from California, she taught at New York University and Harvard University before joining the faculty at UC Davis in 2016.</p>
