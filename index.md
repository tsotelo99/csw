    <h1>Newberry Library Consortium in American Indian Studies</h1>
    <p>The University of New Mexico joined the <a href="https://www.newberry.org/newberry-consortium-american-indian-studies">Newberry Library Consortium in American Indian Studies</a> (NCAIS) in 2010. Many faculty and students have since benefited from annual workshops, institutes, conferences, and fellowships available through the member institutions, along with access to the Newberry Library’s world-renowned collections in American Indian and Indigenous studies and the resources of the D’Arcy McNickle Center.</p>

          <h3>Research access and collaboration</h3>
          <p>In June 2008, the Newberry inaugurated a new Consortium in American Indian Studies. NCAIS began accepting members in the summer and launched its programs in July 2009.</p>
          <p>The consortium draws on the Newberry’s world-renowned collections in American Indian and Indigenous studies and supports a wide range of scholarly opportunities.</p>
